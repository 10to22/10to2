Press release

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

Fort Collins, CO

Fort Collins producer 10to2 makes electronic music in a mostly-guitar town, and is helping build a studio for the next kid

At 19, Finn Cryan is an unlikely fixture in the Fort Collins, Colorado music scene. Under the name 10to2, he makes maximalist hyperpop and electronic music in a state mostly known for guitars, banjos and open fields. In April 2026 he played FoCoMX at The Lyric, and off stage he helped found Noisewatt, a nonprofit recording studio still in its very early phases. Started by young musicians to give local artists an affordable, professional room to record in, it is very much a work in progress and still being built, not a finished or fully operational space.

Cryan is largely self-taught. He has been making songs on a laptop for years, and more recently, in the last year or two, dug deep into audio engineering and how it actually works. He has since completed music-production and music-business certificate programs from Berklee College of Music and audio engineering coursework from the University of Rochester, and he now puts that knowledge to work engineering sessions for other artists at Noisewatt.

Beyond his own releases, Cryan has built up a steady run of collaborations and production credits, working with artists including Bigbabygucci, Funeral, Angelus, Raftheartist, Tsuyonoshi, Doxia and DJ ESS, and he recently closed for DJ Dave at a Denver show.

“I really like making more electronic music in a state that’s mostly banjos, guitars, tractors and fields,” says Cryan. “The fields are great, honestly. They’re really underrated as venues.”

10to2’s catalog includes Whoareyou?, One2many, Ifwecrash and 1 Shot Roulette, available on Spotify and Apple Music. Cryan also performs in a rock band that sits closer to indie rock, moving between the two worlds with the same DIY ethic.

About 10to2
10to2 is the hyperpop/electronic project of Finn Cryan, a 19-year-old producer and audio engineer from Fort Collins, Colorado. A self-taught artist with certificate programs from Berklee College of Music and audio engineering training from the University of Rochester, he is a co-founder of Noisewatt, a nonprofit studio still in its very early phases and being built out, and played FoCoMX 2026. His collaborations and production credits include work with Bigbabygucci, Funeral, Angelus, Raftheartist, Tsuyonoshi, Doxia and DJ ESS. Learn more at 10to2.net.

Long (~200 words)

10to2 is the hyperpop and electronic project of Finn Cryan, a 19-year-old producer and audio engineer based in Fort Collins, Colorado. Drawn to maximalist, emotional pop built on fast electronic production, he stands out in a local scene that mostly plays guitars, a contrast he embraces and one he blurs further by also performing in a rock band that sits closer to indie rock. Cryan is largely self-taught. He was making songs on a laptop long before he had a name for what he was doing, and it was only in the last year or two that he dug into how audio engineering actually works. He later formalized that instinct with certificates from Berklee College of Music in Electronic Music Production, The Business of Music Production and Music Business, plus audio engineering coursework from the University of Rochester and Dante Level 2 certification. Alongside his own catalog he has picked up a growing list of features and production credits, working with artists including Bigbabygucci, Funeral, Angelus, Raftheartist, Tsuyonoshi, Doxia and DJ ESS, and he recently closed for DJ Dave in Denver. In 2026 he played FoCoMX at The Lyric and co-founded Noisewatt, a nonprofit recording studio still in its very early phases, started by young musicians to give Fort Collins artists an affordable, acoustically treated room to record, mix and master in. Noisewatt is still being built and is very much a work in progress rather than a finished studio. His catalog, including Whoareyou?, One2many, Ifwecrash and 1 Shot Roulette, is available on Spotify and Apple Music, with more on the way.
