FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

Fort Collins, CO

Fort Collins producer 10to2 brings hyperpop to a guitar town — and co-builds a studio for the next kid

At 19, Finn Cryan is an unlikely fixture in Fort Collins, Colorado’s mostly-rock music scene. Under the name 10to2, he makes maximalist hyperpop and electronic music — glossy, fast, emotional — in a city better known for guitars. In 2026 he played FoCoMX at The Lyric, and off stage he helped found Noisewatt, a nonprofit recording studio started by young musicians to give local artists an affordable, professional room to record in.

Cryan is largely self-taught, having started making songs on a laptop years before formal training. He has since completed music-production and music-business specializations from Berklee College of Music and audio engineering coursework from the University of Rochester, and he now puts that knowledge to work engineering sessions for other artists at Noisewatt.

“I make hyperpop in a town that mostly plays guitars — I like being the odd one out,” says Cryan. “Co-founding Noisewatt was about building the room I wish I’d had at 15. Everything I learned, I taught myself; I just want the next kid to start with a door already open.”

10to2’s catalog includes Whoareyou?, One2many and Ifwecrash, available on Spotify and Apple Music. Cryan also performs in a shoegaze-leaning rock band, moving between the two worlds with the same DIY ethic.

About 10to2

A self-taught artist with specializations from Berklee College of Music and audio engineering training from the University of Rochester, he is a co-founder of the nonprofit studio Noisewatt and played FoCoMX 2026. Long (~200 words)

10to2 is the hyperpop and electronic project of Finn Cryan, a 19-year-old producer and audio engineer based in Fort Collins, Colorado. Drawn to maximalist, emotional pop built on fast electronic production, he stands out in a local scene that leans heavily on guitars — a contrast he embraces, and one he blurs further by also performing in a shoegaze-leaning rock band. Cryan is largely self-taught: he was making songs on a laptop long before he had a name for what he was doing. He later formalized that instinct with specializations from Berklee College of Music in Electronic Music Production, The Business of Music Production and Music Business, plus audio engineering coursework from the University of Rochester and Dante Level 2 certification. In 2026 he played FoCoMX at The Lyric and co-founded Noisewatt, a nonprofit recording studio started by young musicians to give Fort Collins artists an affordable, acoustically treated room to record, mix and master in — the kind of space Cryan says he wished he’d had starting out. His catalog, including Whoareyou?, One2many and Ifwecrash, is available on Spotify and Apple Music, with more on the way.
